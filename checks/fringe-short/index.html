What the rule says

Base rate plus fringe. A dated summary, not legal advice. Sources read 8 November 2026. Example checked 10 November 2026.

29 CFR 5.31(b) allows the fringe obligation to be met with plan payments, cash in place of fringe, or both. The full base-plus-fringe total still has to be covered for each hour.

A wage at the base rate does not contribute any extra cash toward fringe. In that case the entered cash fringe and plan credit must supply the fringe amount. The checker calls the remaining gap Fringe short.

and by making an additional cash payment in lieu of the required benefits

A week that trips it

The worker names, trades and rates are made up and illustrative. Both result boxes below are printed from the free checker's output for the displayed CSV and rate table.

Dee F. receives the sample Laborer base wage for every recorded hour. The cash fringe entry is smaller than the sample fringe requirement and there is no plan credit. A wage-only review would see a matching base rate while missing this separate part of the hourly obligation.

The week has no premium hours. Each day therefore contributes the same kind of gap to the weekly figure. The checker shows how much fringe credit it found, rather than assuming that a blank plan field stands for a benefit payment made elsewhere.

Fringe short

Dee F., Laborer: the fringe is $6.00 an hour. Cash above the base rate, cash fringe and plan payments come to $4.00 an hour. That's $2.00 an hour short, about $80.00 this week.

The fix

Keep the wage at $22.00 and raise cash fringe from $4.00 to $6.00. The entered $4.00 of credit against $6.00 due leaves $2.00 missing each hour. Multiplying that gap by 40 hours gives $80.00. The corrected export supplies the full fringe in cash and needs no plan credit.

A different real payroll could use a supported plan payment instead. Do not count the same payment twice in both fringe columns. Work from the actual split between cash in lieu and plan contributions, then run the week again with that split.

What the checker doesn't check here

This comparison accepts hourly benefit credits without checking plan terms, funding, payment dates or annualization. Nonhourly fringe requirements need to be turned into hourly amounts by the user before entry.

No part of this result reviews gross wages, deductions, rebates or the signed form. A clear fringe result speaks only to the entered rates and hours.

Questions about this check

Must all fringe be paid into a plan?

No. 29 CFR 5.31(b) lists cash in place of fringe, plan payments, and a combination as ways to meet the obligation.

Is an above-base wage counted here?

Yes. The checker counts the cash wage above the base toward fringe, along with cash fringe and plan payments.

Can the checker validate an hourly plan credit?

No. The page takes plan_fringe as typed and cannot check whether the credit is annualized or the plan qualifies.
