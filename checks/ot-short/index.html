What the rule says

Overtime. A dated summary, not legal advice. Sources read 8 November 2026. Example checked 10 November 2026.

40 U.S.C. 3702(a) requires at least one and a half times the basic rate of pay for hours over 40 in the workweek. Fringe contributions and cash in place of fringe are excluded from that basis and paid at their straight amount.

The basic rate used for overtime cannot fall below the wage determination's base. The checker compares an entered overtime wage with its calculated minimum and flags a gap of at least one cent an hour.

wages include compensation, at a rate not less than one and one-half times the basic rate of pay, for all hours worked in excess of 40 hours in the workweek.

40 U.S.C. 3702(a). Source read 8 November 2026.

in no event can the regular or basic rate upon which premium pay for overtime is calculated ... be less than the amount determined by the Secretary of Labor as the basic hourly rate (i.e. cash rate)

29 CFR 5.32(a). Source read 8 November 2026.

Cal D. works five nine-hour days. The regular wage and plan credit meet the sample hourly obligation, but the overtime wage field repeats the straight-time wage. This isolates an overtime gap without creating a separate fringe shortage in the same week.

Overtime under time and a half

Cal D., Carpenter: 5 hours of overtime paid at $30.00 an hour. Overtime needs at least $45.00 an hour, not counting cash fringe. That's 1.5 times the $30.00 basic rate. You're $15.00 an hour short, about $75.00 this week.

The fix

Change ot_rate_paid from $30.00 to $45.00 while keeping the $10.00 plan credit on every hour. The recorded week leaves 5 hours over 40. One and a half times the $30.00 basis is $45.00. The $15.00 hourly wage gap across those hours produces the $75.00 shortfall in the first result.

The plan credit is not multiplied by one and a half. The corrected table leaves it unchanged and records the higher overtime wage separately. Check an actual export against the payroll records so that regular and overtime wage fields describe what was paid.

What the checker doesn't check here

Hours worked off this project during the same week are absent and can change the overtime count. This page cannot review a worker split across trades and does not decide whether a contract meets the CWHSSA dollar threshold.

The default mode uses the federal weekly hours test. It does not supply federal daily overtime or the FLSA's own coverage tests. Michigan mode has a separate standard schedule.

Questions about this check

Does fringe get multiplied for overtime?

No. 29 CFR 5.32(a) excludes fringe amounts from the overtime rate, so fringe contributions and cash paid in place of fringe are paid at their straight amount.

Does exactly 40 hours create federal overtime here?

No. The weekly test in 40 U.S.C. 3702(a) covers hours over 40, so exactly 40 has no overtime hours.

Can hours off the site count toward overtime?

Yes. DOL's WH-347 instructions count hours worked on and off the site toward overtime in the workweek. The file shows only the hours entered in it.
