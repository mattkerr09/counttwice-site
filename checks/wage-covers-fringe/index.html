Does cash pay above the Davis-Bacon base count toward fringe?

Yes, the checker counts the part of the cash wage above base toward the fringe and shows that credit in a note.

What the rule says

Base rate plus fringe. A dated summary, not legal advice. Sources read 8 November 2026. Example checked 10 November 2026.

Under 29 CFR 5.31(b), cash paid above the basic hourly rate counts toward fringe. The requirement is tested on the combined hourly total, so a separate fringe column is not the only possible source of credit.

The note identifies how much of the entered wage was needed to cover fringe after the cash-fringe and plan columns. It does not add a payment or create a weekly loss.

The fix

In the comparison, the supported cash split is entered as a $30.00 wage plus $10.00 cash fringe. This adds to the same $40.00 hourly cash payment as the first table. The same hours at that same total preserve the entered cash value for the week. With no above-base wage left to credit, the note clears.

The split must reflect a payment that really is in place of fringe. Do not move an ordinary wage amount to another column just to change overtime math. The first result remains a valid illustration of the total test even if a contractor keeps the combined wage field.

What the checker doesn't check here

The page cannot establish the purpose of a cash payment from its label. For overtime, the checker takes the user's wage and in-lieu split as an input and uses a strict full-wage basis when the amount remains in rate_paid.

No plan eligibility review is performed. If a real worker also has benefits, enter the supported hourly plan credit and avoid treating the same dollars as both cash and plan payments.

Questions about this check

Is a separate fringe payment always needed?

No. Under 29 CFR 5.31(b), cash wages above the base can count toward fringe, and cash and plan payments can be combined.

What if the plan already covers the whole fringe?

Then there is no wage credit to show. The checker's wage-credit note applies only when cash fringe plus plan payments are still below fringe and the wage is above base.

Could a combined wage affect overtime?

Yes. The checker uses the full wage field for overtime unless the in-lieu part is entered separately as cash_fringe.
