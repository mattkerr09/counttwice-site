What these rates show

Alabama building bids need room for both survey and union carpentry. The survey base is $14.01 with $0.00 fringe in AL20260078, from a decision covering Barbour and 7 other counties. At AL20260096, a decision covering Shelby County, the union line pays $32.35 plus $11.95. The higher base is more than twice the lower one. That gap makes a single allowance for carpenters across Alabama a poor way to price these contracts.

Plumbing has a similar split, but its benefit cost also rises sharply. AL20260084, a decision covering Calhoun County, pairs a survey base of $18.97 with $0.36 fringe. The union plumber line in AL20260076, a decision covering Marshall County, requires $36.10 and $15.95. A bid based only on the difference in cash wages would miss much of the change. Carry the benefit obligation from the same plumbing line into the estimate.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
