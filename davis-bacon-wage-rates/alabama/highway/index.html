What these rates show

On Alabama highway work, the electrical allowance can change even when the road crews have similar duties. The survey electrician rate in AL20260149, a decision covering Calhoun County, is $19.73 with $0.00 fringe. The union line at AL20260147, a decision covering Autauga County, pays $30.90 and $10.34. These are highway sources. Do not fill an electrical item from a building schedule just because another Alabama bid used it.

The concrete comparison is narrower and both ends come from survey rates. Cement masons receive $13.561 plus $0.00 in AL20260160, a decision covering Lee County, against $18.72 plus $0.00 at AL20260168, a decision covering Shelby County. There is no dollar fringe at either end of that comparison. The base still changes enough to matter when the paving or structure quantities involve many hours. Equipment groups have their own duties in the table below.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
