What these rates show

California heavy and highway plumbing needs a close reading of both county limits and work limits. The lower union plumber and steamfitter line pays $51.65 plus $41.59 fringe for a decision covering Tulare County in CA20260021. The upper union line is new construction at $91.00 plus $49.40 for Lake and Mendocino counties in CA20260004.

The electrical difference also comes from union lines, rather than a survey-to-union change. CA20260007, Modoc and Siskiyou counties, lists $47.04 plus $21.84 fringe. For San Francisco County, CA20260018 gives $91.25 with $48.05. The county beside the electrical line matters more than the first county on the decision. The California equipment details include crane and derrick-barge work with tonnage limits, plus separate drilling and earth-moving descriptions. A heavy and highway equipment estimate needs the machine's capacity and task alongside its published rate.

A dated summary of the Labor Department's published wage determinations, read 11 November 2026. Not legal advice. Always use the determination in your contract.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
