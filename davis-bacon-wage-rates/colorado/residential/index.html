What these rates show

Colorado residential plumbing ranges from a survey line with modest fringe to a union line whose benefit amount is much larger. CO20260005, a decision covering Alamosa and 51 other counties, lists $18.06 plus $2.78; CO20260004 lists $41.32 plus $16.01 for Mesa County. The upper cash wage is more than twice the lower one. Residential work does not make that union obligation disappear. Choose the contract's source before pricing plumbing hours for a house or another covered residential project.

Electrical pay moves differently from plumbing: both residential electrical limits are union lines. CO20260004 gives $30.53 plus $16.30 fringe for El Paso County. CO20260005 gives $36.95 plus $14.41 for Dolores, Gunnison, Hinsdale, La Plata, Moffat, Montezuma, Ouray, Pitkin, Routt, San Juan and San Miguel counties in Colorado.. The high base has the lower fringe of these two lines. That is why choosing the largest base and the largest fringe separately would create an obligation not printed on either source line. Pair the amounts that actually appear together.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
