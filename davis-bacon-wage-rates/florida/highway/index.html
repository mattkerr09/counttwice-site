What these rates show

Florida highway carpenter limits are survey rates with zero fringes at both ends. FL20260003, a decision covering Columbia County, gives $14.98 plus $0.00, and FL20260243, a decision covering Citrus County, gives $24.96 plus $0.00. The higher base is still well above the lower base. A road estimate needs the county decision even when the benefit columns look identical. These highway carpenter lines are separate from the union and survey building comparison elsewhere in the Florida summaries.

The survey general laborer range has the same zero-fringe pattern, but different cash amounts. FL20260237, a decision covering Calhoun County, prints $12.001 with $0.00 fringe. FL20260266, a decision covering Pinellas County, prints $19.99 with $0.00. The upper cash rate is substantially higher, so a benefit column of zero is not evidence of level labor costs across Florida roads.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
