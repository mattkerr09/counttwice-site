What these rates show

Illinois building carpenter limits are union rates, with benefits adding heavily at both ends. The lower line in IL20260002, Crawford County, is $32.14 plus $25.24 fringe. IL20260008 gives $52.01 plus $38.85 for DuPage Ane Lake counties. This is a regional difference within union carpentry, rather than a survey-to-union change. Keep the county wording beside the carpenter line when costing a building crew, since the decision can cover more places than an individual rate.

Electrical work has a wider base spread than carpentry. IL20260001, Quincy Division - Adams, Brown, Hancock, McDonough (Lamoine, Bethel, Industry & Eldorado), Pike, and Schuyler counties, prints the union electrician at $35.00 plus $20.67. The upper union line at IL20260001, Boone, Carroll (Cherry Grove, Shannon, Rock Creek, Lina, Wysox & Elkhorn Grove Twps), DeKalb (Franklin, Kingston, Genoa, South Grove, Mansfield, DeKalb, Corland, Milan, Alton Pierce, Shabbona Mayfield, Sycamore, Malta, Paw Paw, Squaw Grove, Victor, & Somonauk Twps), Jo Daviess (Warren & Rush), Lee, Ogle, Stephenson, Whiteside (Genesee, Jordan, Hopkins, Sterling, Hume, Montgomery, Tampico, & Hahnaman Twps), and Winnebago counties, pays $62.61 plus $43.96. Its fringe also exceeds the lower electrical line's benefit amount. The upper decision's broad county coverage is only a source locator. Use its full electrical classification and any area limits before carrying that high amount into an Illinois building estimate.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
