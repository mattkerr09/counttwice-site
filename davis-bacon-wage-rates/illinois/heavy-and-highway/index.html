What these rates show

Illinois heavy and highway electrical rates differ widely between union lines. IL20260015 gives $35.00 plus $20.67 fringe for Adams County. IL20260011, Boone (all) & DeKalb (remainder) counties, has the upper base of $62.61 with $43.96. A union source label therefore does not produce one statewide electrical allowance. The lower rate has a county note on its own line, while the upper quote needs the full decision review to establish its exact reach. Keep that distinction in the heavy and highway bid. The electrical technician specialty has a long description covering communication and signal systems. Its opening words are a reason to read further before assigning a worker to that entry. Radio, telephone, data and fibre optic equipment appear in its duties. The general electrician comparison cannot settle whether a particular installation belongs there. Save the complete scope with the estimate when the planned work involves those systems. A short crew label such as electrical does not preserve that distinction for the next person reviewing the bid.

Concrete work has a large benefit obligation as well as a wide cash spread. The union cement mason in IL20260014, Henderson (northern half) and Mercer (except southeastern corner) counties, carries $28.52 plus $24.04. At IL20260009, a decision covering Cook County, the union line carries $53.10 and $43.80. The high fringe is nearly as large as the high base. For labor-heavy concrete work, pricing only the cash difference would miss a substantial cost. Read the concrete duties separately from the equipment groups used alongside the crew.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
