What these rates show

Kentucky highway electrical work has union sources with a cash and benefit gap. KY20260040, Fulton County (Up to a 5 Mile Radius of City Hall in Fulton), lists $28.60 plus $15.49 fringe. The union rate for Boyd, Carter, Elliot & Rowan counties in KY20260038 is $41.15 plus $29.35. Keep that county group with the electrical amount. A decision can cover many counties while assigning a narrower group to a trade, so the decision's first county is not reliable evidence for where this electrician line applies.

Plumbing also compares a broad survey source with a union line that prints its own county list. The survey line in KY20260107, a decision covering Adair and 41 other counties, pays $22.52 plus $7.80. The union plumber in KY20260040 pays $44.26 plus $20.28 for Ballard, Caldwell, Calloway, Carlisle, Christian, Crittenden, Fulton, Graves, Hickman, Livingston, Lyon, Marshall, McCracken and Trigg counties. The base is nearly twice the survey base, with more fringe as well. Before costing highway plumbing hours, match the full list and duties rather than using a short county label copied from the decision heading.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
