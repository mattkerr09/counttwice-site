What these rates show

Michigan heavy general laborer pay shows a particularly wide survey-to-union base spread. MI20260040, a decision covering Montcalm County, lists $11.151 plus $4.31 fringe. The union line in MI20260066, a decision covering Monroe County, lists $38.58 plus $15.70. The upper cash requirement is more than three times the lower one, with a larger benefit amount as well.

The plumbing comparison is also survey to union, but its high fringe is larger than the high laborer fringe. MI20260035, a decision covering Emmet County, gives $17.53 base and $4.17 fringe. MI20260044, Lenawee County (townships of Clinton, Macon & Tecumseh), gives $44.31 and $23.70. Its upper source covers several counties, so it is described as decision coverage rather than assigning the plumber rate to the first county. The full line's duties and any narrower local conditions still need to match the Michigan heavy contract. The Michigan heavy details name crane work and backhoe or excavator work under operators. Truck descriptions distinguish tractor haul trucks from dump trucks. Use the planned digging, lifting and hauling tasks to choose the source lines for that crew.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
