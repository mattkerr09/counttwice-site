What these rates show

Michigan highway plumber limits both come from union lines in a broadly covering decision. MI20260001, a decision covering Alcona and 82 other counties, gives $24.19 plus $12.28 fringe; MI20260001, a decision covering Alcona and 82 other counties, gives $33.03 plus $20.19. Both components increase at the upper end. The shared decision number does not make the lines interchangeable. Read the local scope and plumbing duties within the highway document before selecting a rate for the road crew, especially when copying an allowance from another Michigan job.

The general laborer comparison has explicit zone markers. MI20260001 lists $28.72 with $18.45 fringe under Zone 4. The upper base in MI20260001 is $32.10 with $17.45 under Zone 2. The high base has the smaller fringe, so the table's separate limits should not be added together as if they formed one printed rate. Use the highway source's zone definitions to locate the work, then keep the chosen laborer base and fringe paired for the estimate.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
