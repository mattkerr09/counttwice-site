What these rates show

Nebraska highway electrical work has a wide survey-to-union gap and an unusually specific boundary on the upper line. NE20260028 lists $14.23 plus $5.95 fringe for Sarpy County. The union rate in NE20260027 is $46.40 plus $20.89 for Saunders County - east of highway 109 and north of alternate highway 30. Roads form part of that area definition. Retain those directions when costing the electrical crew, rather than shortening the union quote to a county-wide rate that the line does not actually state.

The carpenter highway comparison stays within survey rates. NE20260030, a decision covering Arthur and 29 other counties, gives $16.39 with $0.00 fringe; NE20260028 gives $25.29 with $8.06 for Douglas County. The upper cash base also adds benefits where the lower pair has zero fringe. One carpenter source has broad county coverage, while the other line names its county directly. Those are different kinds of location evidence. Read the complete work line and source before applying the amount to a Nebraska road project.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
