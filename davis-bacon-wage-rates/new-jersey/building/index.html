What these rates show

New Jersey building carpentry has one included general union base in this source set. The first source line, NJ20260025, a decision covering Atlantic County, gives $57.42 plus $34.17 fringe. The table has no wider general carpenter cash range, but that does not collapse drywall or other named specialty work into this line. The benefit obligation is also substantial despite the flat base. Review the carpenter duties and the contract's decision before treating the common figure as an allowance for every building carpentry task.

Electrical pay is much less uniform than the carpenter base. The lower union electrician in NJ20260027 has $45.51 plus $34.39 fringe, with the restriction Atlantic County; Burlington County (south of a line following the west and south limits of Burlington Borough from the Delaware River, in a southeasterly direction, to the Burlington - Mt. Holly road; then, south-southeast along the Burlington - Mt. Holly road to the town of Mt. Holly, does not include Mt. Holly; then, east along the Pennsylvania Railroad to the town of New Lisbon, does not include New Lisbon; then, continuing along the Pennsylvania Railroad to the Ocean county line); Camden, Cape May, Cumberland, Gloucester and Salem counties. The upper union line at NJ20260031, a decision covering Essex County, has $73.59 plus $45.99. The lower boundary follows towns, roads and rail lines, not simply a county. Keep that full restriction with the quote. Shortening it to a decision's first county would erase geography that the electrical source explicitly makes relevant.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
