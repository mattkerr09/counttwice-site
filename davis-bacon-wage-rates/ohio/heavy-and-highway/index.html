What these rates show

Ohio heavy and highway plumber limits are union lines in one broadly covering decision. OH20260001, a decision covering Adams and 87 other counties, lists $35.94 plus $37.35 fringe. The higher cash line in OH20260001, a decision covering Adams and 87 other counties, lists $53.00 with $27.59. Its fringe is lower than the fringe beside the low base. Compare the paired obligations in the full source rather than adding the largest values from separate columns. The shared decision number also leaves the work and local notes inside the document to distinguish the lines. One contract source can contain several local plumbing obligations. Locate the applicable work description inside it before choosing between the cash amounts shown here.

Electrical base pay varies within that union heavy and highway decision as well. OH20260001, Ashland, Crawford, Huron (Richmond, New Haven, Ripley & Greenwich townships), Knox (Liberty, Clinton, Union, Howard, Monroe, Middleberry, Morris, Wayne, Berlin, Pike, Brown & Jefferson townships), Marion, Morrow, Richland and Wyandot (Sycamore, Crane, Eden, Pitt, Antrim & Tymochtee townships) counties, gives $32.30 with $21.83 fringe, against $48.40 plus $25.24 in OH20260001, a decision covering Adams and 87 other counties. Here the upper base also has the upper fringe of the two quotes. That is different from the plumbing pattern. An Ohio heavy and highway estimate needs each trade's own pair, plus its complete classification and any narrower geographic restriction, even when both crews are paid from the same decision.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
