What these rates show

Pennsylvania heavy and highway electrical limits can more than double within one union decision. PA20260004, Delaware County :(that portion south of U.S. Highway No. 1 and west of U.S. Highway No. 202) Chester County (that portion south and east of U. S. Highway 1), gives $35.00 plus $23.70 fringe; its upper line in PA20260004, a decision covering Bucks and 4 other counties, gives $75.05 plus $47.02. The benefit amount also nearly doubles. A decision number alone therefore cannot select the electrician rate. Read the complete electrical duties and local restrictions inside the heavy and highway source, rather than assigning every line to its first county or assuming one amount applies throughout the broad coverage.

Plumbing has a narrower heavy and highway cash spread, but one lower line uses a local remainder restriction. PA20260018, marked Columbia (remainder of county), Cumberland, Dauphin, Juniata, Lancaster, Lebanon (west of Route 501), Montour, Northumberland, Perry, Snyder, York, Bradford County (western half), Sullivan, Tioga and Union counties, prints $41.36 with $29.45 fringe. The upper union line in PA20260004, a decision covering Bucks and 4 other counties, prints $73.73 with $39.86. Read what the remainder means in the surrounding decision text before using that lower amount. A shortened county label could lose the excluded part of the area. Keep that restriction with the plumber pair in the heavy and highway estimate.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
