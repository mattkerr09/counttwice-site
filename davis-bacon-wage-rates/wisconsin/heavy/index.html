What these rates show

Wisconsin heavy general laborer limits have a low union base paired with fringe larger than that base. WI20260008 lists $19.99 plus $24.42 fringe for Milwaukee, Ozaukee, Washington, and Waukesha counties. The upper union base in WI20260015, a decision covering Adams and 71 other counties, is $43.77 with $19.97. Cash more than doubles while fringe falls. A base-only comparison would overstate the change in paired cost. The lower county group is printed with the laborer line and should stay with the quote when preparing the heavy bid.

Electrical pay has a different union pattern: both the base and fringe increase at the upper end. WI20260008, Adams, Clark (Colby, Freemont, Lynn, Mayville, Sherman, Sherwood, Unity), Forest, Juneau, Langlade, Lincoln, Marathon, Marinette (Beecher, Dunbar, Goodman & Pembine), Menominee (Area west of a line 6 miles west of the west boundary of Oconto County), Oneida, Portage, Shawano (Aniwa and Hutchins), Vilas and Wood counties, gives $40.19 base with $22.90 fringe. The union line in WI20260008 gives $50.86 plus $28.26 for Milwaukee, Ozaukee, Washington, and Waukesha counties. The upper county group is the line's own area note, not a place inferred from the decision heading. Read the electrician's local divisions separately from the laborer's, even when both rates appear in the same Wisconsin heavy document.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
