What these rates show

Wisconsin highway electrical estimates need both a local division and the value of the electrical contract. The lower general union line in WI20260010 pays $40.19 plus $22.90 fringe. Its work description splits several counties into smaller areas. The upper line in WI20260010 gives $50.86 plus $28.26 for Milwaukee, Ozaukee, Washington, and Waukesha counties. Electrical contracts above and below a stated dollar value appear in the specialty list. Read that work limit and the source's county boundaries before choosing a rate for a highway crew.

Cement mason fringes are close across the highway union limits even though cash bases differ. The lower line at WI20260010, Area F, is $37.33 with $31.38 fringe. The upper line at WI20260010, Area A, is $47.22 with $31.90. Both benefit amounts are substantial relative to cash. The concrete pair has a smaller base spread than the electrical pair above. Use the complete concrete duties and local notes inside the Wisconsin decision before treating either cement mason limit as the road crew's rate.

How to use these rates

Pay the rate for the work actually done, whatever the worker's skill. A worker who does two trades can be paid each trade's rate for its hours only if the payroll records accurately show the time spent in each (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(i)).

The basic hourly rate is the cash wage. The fringe is a second amount owed for every hour. It can go into bona fide benefit plans, be paid in cash on top of the base, or be a mix, as long as the total is at least base plus fringe (40 U.S.C. 3142(d); 29 CFR 5.31(b)).

A trade the work needs but the decision doesn't list is added only through the contract's conformance process (29 CFR 5.5(a)(1)(iii)).
