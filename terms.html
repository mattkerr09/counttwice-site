Terms

Count Twice is free to use, with no account. Using it means you agree to these terms.

What it does

Count Twice checks the numbers you enter against the rates you type. It does the math on one week of payroll and lists what looks short. It doesn't look up your wage determination, decide which trade fits the work, or check anything the home page lists under What it doesn't check.

It isn't legal advice

Count Twice isn't legal advice, and it isn't a payroll service or an accountant. Using it doesn't make anyone your lawyer or adviser. If something doesn't fit your job, ask the contracting officer or the agency named in your contract, or a lawyer.

You remain responsible

You remain responsible for your certified payroll: the rates, the hours, the pay, the WH-347 or your state's filing, and the Statement of Compliance you sign. A check with no flags doesn't mean the payroll is right. It means nothing came up short on the checks this page runs.

The math is tested, but the page can still have mistakes, and the rules can change after the date shown on the page. Count Twice is provided as is, without warranties of any kind.
